### Do you need a commercial license?
If your company plans to:
1. Use the system within proprietary (closed-source) software.
2. Offer the system as a service (SaaS) without the obligation to publish the source code of your own modifications.
3. Require dedicated technical support (SLA) and stability guarantees.

...then the AGPLv3 license may pose a restriction for you. We offer full support for transitioning to a commercial model.

### What we offer to business clients:
- **Commercial License:** Complete exemption from the requirements of the AGPLv3 license. You receive the right to use, modify, and redistribute the system in a closed-source format.

- **Proprietary Modules:** Access to exclusive drivers, optimized system kernels, and remote management tools that are not publicly available.
- **Certification:** Official verification of your solution's compatibility with our system.
